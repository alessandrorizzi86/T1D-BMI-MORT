### T1D-BMI-MORT
**Author**: Alessandro Rizzi

**Email**: ar671@leicester.ac.uk

**Project Description**
Evaluation of the association between body mass index (BMI) groups, defined according to the World Health Organisation, and all-cause mortality and cause-specific mortality in subjects with type 1 diabetes (T1D) and matched controls using UK Clinical Practice Research Datalink (CPRD) GOLD and Aurum.
